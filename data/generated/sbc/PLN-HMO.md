# ClaimSense HMO Select — Summary of Benefits and Coverage

Plan ID PLN-HMO · HMO · coverage period starting 2026-01-01. Fictional plan for the ClaimSense prototype (synthetic data).

## Important questions

- **Overall deductible:** $250.00 per person in network. Out-of-network care is not covered.
- **Services covered before you meet the deductible:** Primary care visit, Specialist visit, Preventive care and screening, Outpatient mental health services, Physical therapy. Copays for these services do not count toward the deductible.
- **Out-of-pocket limit:** $4,000.00 per person in network.
- **What counts toward the out-of-pocket limit:** deductible, copays and coinsurance. Amounts above the allowed amount billed by out-of-network providers do not count.
- **Benefit period:** calendar year; the deductible and out-of-pocket totals reset on 1 January.
- **Do you need a referral to see a specialist?** Yes. You need a referral from your primary care provider to see: allergy immunology, cardiology, gastroenterology, neurology, ophthalmology, pain medicine, physical therapy, plastic surgery, podiatry, psychology, radiology, sleep medicine, vascular surgery, wound care.
- **Does this plan use a provider network?** Yes. Only in-network providers are covered.

## What you pay for covered services

| Service | In-network cost | Out-of-network cost | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary care visit | $20.00 copay per service; deductible does not apply | Not covered | None |
| Specialist visit | $40.00 copay per service; deductible does not apply | Not covered | None |
| Preventive care and screening | No charge; deductible does not apply | Not covered | None |
| Advanced imaging (CT, MRI) | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | Prior authorization required |
| Diagnostic tests (heart, sleep, nerve, allergy, ultrasound) | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | Prior authorization required |
| Outpatient mental health services | $20.00 copay per service; deductible does not apply | Not covered | None |
| Pain management injections | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | Prior authorization required |
| Outpatient surgery | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | Prior authorization required |
| Endoscopy (colonoscopy) | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | None |
| Minor procedures (foot care, wound care) | 20% coinsurance after deductible | Not covered | None |
| Physical therapy | $30.00 copay per service; deductible does not apply | Not covered | Prior authorization required; Limited to 20 visits per calendar year |

## Prior authorization

Your provider must get approval from the plan before these services, or the claim is denied: Advanced imaging (CT, MRI), Diagnostic tests (heart, sleep, nerve, allergy, ultrasound), Pain management injections, Outpatient surgery, Physical therapy.

## Excluded services

- Skin patch allergy tests, per patch (Patch allergy testing is not a plan benefit)
- Cosmetic procedures, and services that do not meet a coverage policy's criteria.

## Filing a claim

In-network providers must file within 90 days of the date of service. Claims filed later are denied.

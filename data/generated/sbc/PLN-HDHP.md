# ClaimSense HSA Saver (high deductible PPO) — Summary of Benefits and Coverage

Plan ID PLN-HDHP · PPO · coverage period starting 2026-01-01. Fictional plan for the ClaimSense prototype (synthetic data).

## Important questions

- **Overall deductible:** $3,200.00 per person in network; $6,400.00 out of network.
- **Services covered before you meet the deductible:** Preventive care and screening. Copays for these services do not count toward the deductible.
- **Out-of-pocket limit:** $6,500.00 per person in network; $13,000.00 out of network.
- **What counts toward the out-of-pocket limit:** deductible, copays and coinsurance. Amounts above the allowed amount billed by out-of-network providers do not count.
- **Benefit period:** calendar year; the deductible and out-of-pocket totals reset on 1 January.
- **Do you need a referral to see a specialist?** No. You can see a specialist without a referral.
- **Does this plan use a provider network?** Yes. You pay less with in-network providers.

## What you pay for covered services

| Service | In-network cost | Out-of-network cost | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary care visit | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Specialist visit | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Preventive care and screening | No charge; deductible does not apply | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Advanced imaging (CT, MRI) | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | Prior authorization required |
| Diagnostic tests (heart, sleep, nerve, allergy, ultrasound) | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Outpatient mental health services | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Pain management injections | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | Prior authorization required |
| Outpatient surgery | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | Prior authorization required |
| Endoscopy (colonoscopy) | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Minor procedures (foot care, wound care) | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | None |
| Physical therapy | 10% coinsurance after deductible | 40% coinsurance after deductible | Limited to 20 visits per calendar year |

## Prior authorization

Your provider must get approval from the plan before these services, or the claim is denied: Advanced imaging (CT, MRI), Pain management injections, Outpatient surgery.

## Excluded services

- No procedure is excluded by name. Services must still meet the plan's coverage policies.
- Cosmetic procedures, and services that do not meet a coverage policy's criteria.

## Filing a claim

In-network providers must file within 90 days of the date of service. Out-of-network claims must be filed within 180 days. Claims filed later are denied.

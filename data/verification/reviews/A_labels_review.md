- Check 1 diagnoses: all of the line's pointed diagnoses (every pointer, not only the first). A line is covered when, for at least one of the procedure's group combinations, each group in it contains at least one of those diagnoses (for example colonoscopy: group 1, or group 2 and group 3). Combinations come from data/curated diagnosis support, which cites the article and groups; covered codes come from the current article version in data/raw/mcd.
- Check 1 uses app/db/raw.read_article_diagnoses, the same reader the generator uses (training/generate/codebook.py), so it is not an independent check of that reader.
- Check 1 DENY side: only lines the record marks as diagnosis-based (scenario_type deny_dx, reason DN_NOT_MEDICALLY_NECESSARY) are checked; criterion-based denials use the same reason code and are check 2's territory. A diagnosis-based denial in another scenario type would be missed.
- Check 5 threshold: 0.5743, the 5th percentile (nearest rank) of the similarity between each MET criterion and its own evidence sentence (n = 4,046); BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 cosine, both texts embedded as passages. A structural cue is tried first: the criterion names a procedure code that is not the line's, or its subject names a word unique to another code's description in the same policy and none unique to the line's own; a subject that begins with the patient names no procedure. Every cue decision is in A_labels_check5_cues.csv.
- Raw MCD procedure rows show [AMA CPT descriptor omitted] in the two descriptor columns (CLAUDE.md: never copy AMA CPT descriptor text).
- Checker self-test: 14/14 planted cases behaved as expected. Caught (8): 1: APPROVE line, diagnosis not covered; 1: PEND line, diagnosis not covered; 1: diagnosis-based DENY, diagnosis covered; 2: APPROVE line with a NOT_MET criterion; 2: PEND line, all criteria MET, no flag; 3: MET evidence not in the notes; 4: criterion that was not retrieved; 5: NOT_APPLICABLE with no related sentence. Correctly not flagged (4): 1: APPROVE line, diagnosis covered; 2: NCCI_BYPASS PEND line, all criteria MET; 5: NOT_APPLICABLE cleared by a structural cue; 5: NOT_APPLICABLE with a related note sentence. On the real MCD lookups: a code in no covered group is flagged (DEC-TR-00002, A53065) (ok); a code from the required group is not flagged (DEC-TR-00002, A53065) (ok).

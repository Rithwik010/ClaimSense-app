## POL-SURG-002.c1
Colonoscopy to investigate symptoms is covered for an imaging abnormality likely to be significant; unexplained bleeding (blood in the stool not from a rectal source, black tarry stool, or a positive stool blood test); unexplained iron-deficiency anemia; or significant unexplained diarrhea with other findings such as weight loss.

Source: LCD L34005 v34, Indications.

## POL-SURG-002.c2
Surveillance is covered for a history of colorectal cancer or polyps, follow-up one year after colorectal cancer surgery, follow-up after removal of a large polyp, and inflammatory bowel disease surveillance (pancolitis for more than 7 years, or left-sided colitis for more than 15 years).

Source: LCD L34005 v34, Indications.

## POL-SURG-002.c3
Patients identified as high risk for colon cancer are eligible for continued screening every 24 months.

Source: LCD L34005 v34, Indications.

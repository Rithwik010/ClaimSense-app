## POL-DX-002.c1
Testing is covered when the history and examination before the test clearly document the need for it, for example limb pain, numbness or tingling, or weakness that suggests a nerve or muscle disorder. A bare rule-out diagnosis is not sufficient.

Type: requirement
Source: LCD L34594 v42, Indications.

## POL-DX-002.c2
These tests are not a first-line test for limb pain caused by a recent injury or an acute bone injury.

Type: exclusion
Source: LCD L34594 v42, Indications.

## POL-DX-002.c3
Routine testing for nerve damage from diabetes or end-stage kidney disease, or testing only to monitor how severe it is or how well treatment works, is not covered.

Type: exclusion
Source: LCD L34594 v42, Limitations.

## POL-DX-002.c4
A nerve conduction study done without needle EMG is usually not medically necessary. It is covered alone only for carpal tunnel syndrome with a high pre-test probability, which the notes must document.

Type: conditional
Applies to: 95907, 95910
Source: LCD L34594 v42 cov-037.

## POL-BH-001.c2
Testing is not covered when no problem with brain or emotional function is suspected, when it consists only of self-administered or self-scored screening tools, or when it is repeated without being needed for a medical decision.

Type: exclusion
Source: LCD L34520 v32, Limitations.

## POL-BH-001.c3
Routine re-evaluation of chronically disabled patients that is not needed for diagnosis or continued treatment is not medically necessary.

Type: exclusion
Source: LCD L34520 v32, Limitations.

## POL-BH-001.c4
Testing is covered when brain or psychological involvement is known or suspected, or when the results will affect the patient's management by confirming or delineating a diagnosis or otherwise informing diagnosis, treatment planning, prognosis or quality of life. An example is telling dementia or brain injury apart from depression when the diagnosis is still uncertain after examination.

Type: requirement
Source: LCD L34520 v32 cov-003.

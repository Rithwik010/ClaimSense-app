## POL-DX-003.c4
Skin prick tests are covered to evaluate IgE-mediated allergy to airborne allergens, foods, stinging-insect venom, certain drugs or chemicals; intradermal tests are covered for the same allergies except foods. The tests must be correlated with the patient's history and examination.

Type: requirement
Applies to: 95004, 95024
Source: LCD L33261 v40 cov-007, cov-008, cov-009, cov-058.

## POL-DX-003.c5
Intradermal testing to diagnose food allergy is not covered.

Type: exclusion
Applies to: 95024
Source: LCD L33261 v40 cov-058, cov-080.

## POL-DX-003.c6
Patch testing is covered to diagnose allergic contact dermatitis, or other dermatitis or skin eruptions where a contact allergen is the likely cause, when there is a clear-cut clinical suspicion of contact allergy and the chemicals tested are relevant to the problem.

Type: requirement
Applies to: 95044
Source: LCD L33261 v40 cov-010, cov-012.

## POL-DX-003.c7
The tests chosen must match the patient's history and physical findings, and the number of tests must be justified. For an initial evaluation of airborne allergens, guidelines justify fewer than 70 prick and 40 intradermal tests; up to 80 patch tests may be needed to diagnose allergic contact dermatitis.

Type: requirement
Source: LCD L33261 v40 cov-055, cov-056.

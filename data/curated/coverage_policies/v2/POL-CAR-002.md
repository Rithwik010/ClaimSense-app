## POL-CAR-002.c4
Recording for up to 48 hours is covered for one or more of: palpitations, fainting or near fainting, dizziness, chest pain, transient ischemic episodes, shortness of breath, or arrhythmias; checking the response to antiarrhythmic drugs; heart attack survivors with an ejection fraction of 40% or less; coronary artery disease with active symptoms, to correlate chest pain with ST-segment changes; other acute and subacute forms of ischemic heart disease; or detecting arrhythmias after an ablation procedure.

Type: requirement
Applies to: 93224
Source: LCD L34636 v38 cov-004, cov-005 to cov-018.

## POL-CAR-002.c5
Continuous recording for more than 48 hours, up to 15 days, is covered to look for symptom-free episodes when checking the response to treatment in a patient treated for a listed condition.

Type: requirement
Applies to: 93241
Source: LCD L34636 v38 cov-004, cov-019.

## POL-CAR-002.c6
30-day event monitoring is covered for arrhythmias, chest pain, fainting or near fainting, dizziness, palpitations, transient ischemic episodes or shortness of breath; to start, revise or stop antiarrhythmic drug therapy; for heart attack survivors; for acute and subacute forms of ischemic heart disease; or for coronary artery disease with active symptoms, to correlate chest pain with ST-segment changes.

Type: requirement
Applies to: 93268
Source: LCD L34636 v38 cov-001, cov-004, cov-020 to cov-032.

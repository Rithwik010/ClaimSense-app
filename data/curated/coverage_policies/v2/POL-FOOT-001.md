## POL-FOOT-001.c1
Routine foot care is covered for nerve damage in the feet (without blood-vessel impairment) severe enough that care by a non-professional would put the patient at risk. The record must document loss of protective sensation: no feeling at 2 or more of 5 sites on either foot with the 5.07 Semmes-Weinstein monofilament.

Type: conditional
Group: indication
Source: LCD L33941 v23, Covered indications.

## POL-FOOT-001.c2
It is covered for patients on blood thinners when the record documents the significant risk of non-professional foot care.

Type: conditional
Group: indication
Source: LCD L33941 v23, Covered indications.

## POL-FOOT-001.c4
Foot care that would otherwise be routine is covered when a systemic condition, such as diabetes, arteriosclerosis obliterans, Buerger's disease, chronic thrombophlebitis or a peripheral neuropathy involving the feet, causes severe circulatory problems or areas of reduced sensation in the legs or feet, so that care by a non-professional would be hazardous. A diagnosis alone does not show the severity: the record must show it, for example by a non-traumatic amputation of the foot or part of it; two Class B findings (absent posterior tibial pulse, absent dorsalis pedis pulse, or advanced trophic changes, three of which are needed); or one Class B and two Class C findings (claudication, temperature changes such as cold feet, edema, paresthesias, burning). For diabetes, chronic thrombophlebitis and the neuropathies marked in the manual, the patient must be under the active care of a doctor of medicine or osteopathy who documents the condition.

Type: conditional
Group: indication
Source: Medicare Benefit Policy Manual Ch. 15 §290.C.3, §290.D, §290.F, §290.G; LCD L33941 v23 cov-000.

## POL-FOOT-001.c5
Without a systemic condition, treatment of mycotic (fungal) toenails is covered only when the physician attending the mycotic condition documents clinical evidence of mycosis of the toenail and, for a patient who can walk, marked limitation of walking, pain, or secondary infection caused by the thickened, dystrophic nail plate; for a patient who cannot walk, pain or secondary infection caused by it.

Type: conditional
Group: indication
Source: Medicare Benefit Policy Manual Ch. 15 §290.C.4; LCD L33941 v23 cov-000.

## POL-FOOT-001.c6
Except in the situations this policy covers, routine foot care, such as cutting or removing corns and calluses or trimming, cutting, clipping or debriding nails, is not covered.

Type: exclusion
Source: Medicare Benefit Policy Manual Ch. 15 §290.B.2; LCD L33941 v23 cov-000.

## POL-CAR-002.c1
Heart rhythm monitoring is covered for symptoms suggesting an arrhythmia: palpitations, fainting or near fainting, dizziness, chest pain, transient ischemic episodes, shortness of breath, or a known arrhythmia.

Source: LCD L34636 v38, Medical necessity, 48-hour recording.

## POL-CAR-002.c2
It is also covered to check the response to antiarrhythmic drugs, for heart attack survivors with an ejection fraction of 40% or less, for coronary artery disease with active symptoms, and to detect arrhythmias after an ablation procedure.

Source: LCD L34636 v38, Medical necessity, 48-hour recording.

## POL-CAR-002.c3
Recording for more than 48 hours (up to 15 days) or 30-day event monitoring is covered when symptoms occur less often than every 48 hours, or to monitor the response to treatment for the listed conditions.

Source: LCD L34636 v38, Medical necessity, extended and 30-day monitoring.

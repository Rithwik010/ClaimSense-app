## POL-SURG-003.c1
Radiofrequency or laser vein ablation is covered only for symptomatic varicose veins of the great or small saphenous veins and their branches that have failed 3 months of conservative therapy.

Source: LCD L34209 v43, Limitations for ablation, item 1.

## POL-SURG-003.c2
Treating veins that cause no symptoms, or symptomatic veins without a 3-month trial of conservative measures (such as compression, exercise, leg elevation or weight loss), is considered cosmetic and not covered, by any technique.

Source: LCD L34209 v43, Limitations for ablation, item 3.

## POL-SURG-003.c3
Treating spider veins (telangiectasias) by any technique is cosmetic and not covered.

Source: LCD L34209 v43, Limitations for ablation, item 4.

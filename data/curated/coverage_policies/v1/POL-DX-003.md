## POL-DX-003.c1
Skin prick and intradermal tests are covered to evaluate IgE-mediated allergy to airborne allergens, foods, stinging-insect venom, certain drugs or chemicals, when the tests are correlated with the patient's history and examination.

Source: LCD L33261 v40, Covered indications, section A.

## POL-DX-003.c2
Patch testing is covered for suspected allergic contact dermatitis, or other dermatitis where a contact allergen is the likely cause.

Source: LCD L33261 v40, Covered indications, section A.

## POL-DX-003.c3
The tests chosen must match the patient's history and physical findings, and the number of tests must be justified. Guideline limits for an initial evaluation are about 70 prick and 40 intradermal tests for airborne allergens, or up to 80 patch tests.

Source: LCD L33261 v40, Covered indications, number of tests.

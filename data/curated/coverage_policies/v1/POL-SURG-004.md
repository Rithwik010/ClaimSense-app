## POL-SURG-004.c1
Eyelid surgery is reconstructive (covered) when it corrects visual impairment caused by excess eyelid skin weighing down the lashes (dermatochalasis), blepharochalasis, drooping of the eyelid (blepharoptosis) or drooping of the brow.

Source: LCD L33944 v27, Indications and limitations.

## POL-SURG-004.c2
The record must document the patient's complaints of functional problems: vision blocked above or to the side, trouble reading because the upper field is lost, or looking through the eyelashes or seeing eyelid skin.

Source: LCD L33944 v27, Indications and limitations, documentation.

## POL-SURG-004.c3
Visual field testing must show a significant loss of upper vision (at least 12 degrees or 30 percent) that the surgery could correct.

Source: LCD L33944 v27, Indications and limitations, visual fields.

## POL-SURG-004.c4
Without documented functional impairment, eyelid surgery is considered cosmetic and is not covered.

Source: LCD L33944 v27, Indications and limitations.

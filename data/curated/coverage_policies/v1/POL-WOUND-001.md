## POL-WOUND-001.c1
Active wound care (debridement) is covered to remove dead tissue and promote healing, performed with direct one-on-one patient contact, following accepted standards of wound treatment.

Source: LCD L38902 v12, Indications.

## POL-WOUND-001.c2
Care must be part of a plan that addresses complicating factors (pressure off-loading, infection, poor circulation, metabolic or nutritional problems), aiming at wound closure or, when healing is not possible, preventing the wound from getting worse.

Source: LCD L38902 v12, Indications.

## POL-WOUND-001.c3
How often debridement is done depends on the wound. Frequent repeated debridement requires a documented reassessment of the treatment plan.

Source: LCD L38902 v12, Indications, frequency.

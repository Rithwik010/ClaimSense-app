## POL-PAIN-002.c1
The patient must have moderate to severe chronic neck or low back pain, mainly in the spine itself (axial), causing a functional deficit measured on a pain or disability scale.

Source: LCD L33930 v49, Covered indications.

## POL-PAIN-002.c2
The pain must have lasted at least 3 months, with documented failure of non-invasive conservative care.

Source: LCD L33930 v49, Covered indications.

## POL-PAIN-002.c3
There must be no untreated nerve-root pain or neurogenic claudication (except from a facet joint cyst), and no other cause of the pain such as fracture, tumor, infection or major deformity.

Source: LCD L33930 v49, Covered indications.

## POL-PAIN-002.c4
Diagnostic facet procedures are done with the intent of proceeding to radiofrequency ablation at that level if they succeed. A second, confirming diagnostic procedure at the same level must be at least 2 weeks after the first.

Source: LCD L33930 v49, Section A, diagnostic procedures.

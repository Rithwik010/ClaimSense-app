## POL-BH-001.c1
Testing is covered when brain or psychological involvement is known or suspected and the results will affect diagnosis, treatment planning or management. An example is telling dementia or brain injury apart from depression when the diagnosis is still uncertain after examination.

Source: LCD L34520 v32, Indications.

## POL-BH-001.c2
Testing is not covered when no problem with brain or emotional function is suspected, when it consists only of self-administered or self-scored screening tools, or when it is repeated without being needed for a medical decision.

Source: LCD L34520 v32, Limitations.

## POL-BH-001.c3
Routine re-evaluation of chronically disabled patients that is not needed for diagnosis or continued treatment is not medically necessary.

Source: LCD L34520 v32, Limitations.
